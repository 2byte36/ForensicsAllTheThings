This is an initial collection of worked examples, not the coverage boundary of the project. General recipes are also sourced from the [reference library](REFERENCES.md); future examples can come from other authors and events.

These are technique mappings, not reproduced challenge solutions. Original posts retain the complete scripts, screenshots, challenge parameters, and flags. The guides summarize reusable workflows; they do not claim every challenge has a standalone solver here.

## Source caveats worth retaining

- **Alice in DFIRland** recovered a setup script: an unintended solve, not a full incident reconstruction.
- **Word di Press** includes answers inferred or guessed when the capture did not expose them. The network guide requires direct evidence before asserting commands or endpoints.
- **gratisan mulu sih** includes guessed fragment counts before later memory inspection. The recovery guide does not present those guesses as a general algorithm.
- **log** records inconsistent login-attempt counts; count the requests and outcomes in your own evidence.
- **Claudio Maximus** corrects an initial AES-GCM hypothesis to AES-CTR based on call sites.
- **wilson disaster** records a timestamp discrepancy between observation and accepted answer.
- Several source writeups disclose AI-assisted analysis. A reported accepted answer is source evidence, not independent validation of every intermediate claim.
- Old services, external malware links, exposed tokens, and challenge accounts are historical context. This reference uses local artifacts and does not depend on accessing those accounts or replaying live malware.
